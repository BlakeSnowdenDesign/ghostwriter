# Business strategy and competitive research
## Thesis
Most AI tools help writers write; we help people who aren't writers become creators. Sell outcomes, not software or tokens. One conversation, one creative universe, multiple deliverables. Three divisions: Author Studio (fiction, nonfiction, business books), Hollywood Studio (scripts, decks, trailers, adaptations), Legacy Studio (memoir, biographies, family history). Target aspiring filmmakers, executives, consultants, families and aspiring authors; later professional writers and producers.

## Six differentiators
1. Creative Interview: natural voice-first adaptive questioning, optional phone later, original recordings, accessible text fallback, profile, multi-contributor interviews. Extract deeper narrative stakes without inventing facts.
2. Living Story Universe: persistent canonical facts, characters, timeline, themes, author voice, visual identity; versioned adaptations with intentional departures; book, film, series, audiobook, documentary, graphic novel, marketing assets.
3. Creative Audition: 5–10 minute intake, three distinct treatments, title/logline/synopsis/sample scene or chapter, optional art/audio. Free capped preview, $19 expanded, $49 treatments, $79 visual package, $99–149 teaser; credit selected purchases toward upgrade.
4. Hollywood development studio: professional script, treatment, 12–20 slide cinematic pitch deck, key art, character concepts, storyboards, 30/60/90s trailer and secure digital pitch room. Sell preparation, never guarantee sale or production. Avoid full film generation initially.
5. Creative Council: specialist agents plus independent critical passes, one primary author voice, customer approvals, original evaluation suite and blind human comparisons. Quality is the differentiator, not agent count.
6. Story Vault + human network: private recordings, provenance, revisions, family contributions, export/deletion, documented human creativity; later editors, ghostwriters, script doctors, designers, voice actors and attorneys as separately scoped services. Records do not guarantee copyright.
